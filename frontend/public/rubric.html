💡 What FailureDNA Implemented (Plain English)

1. High-Complexity Industrial Time-Series

We used NASA C-MAPSS FD004, the most difficult subset. It contains 249 training engines and 248 test engines across 6 complex flight operating conditions (altitude, Mach, throttle) with dual simultaneous failure modes (HPC compressor and Fan degradation).

2. Zero-Leakage Regime-Aware Feature Engineering

Flight conditions cause huge sensor shifts that fool regular models. We built a RegimeAwarePreprocessor that clusters flight conditions into 6 operating regimes and standardizes sensors per regime using only the first 20 healthy cycles to guarantee zero future data leakage.

3. Chronological Left-Edge Padding & Attention Masking

Our model requires 50-cycle windows. For short test engines (e.g. Unit #098 with only 19 cycles), we implemented left-side edge padding with a binary validity mask so fake padded cycles never pollute the representation.

4. 25+ Publication Visualizations

Generated in reports/figures/: sensor correlation matrices, 3D operating regime clusters, longitudinal degradation profiles, t-SNE latent manifold, and residual distributions.

💡 What FailureDNA Implemented (Plain English)

1. 4-Layer Pre-LayerNorm Temporal Transformer

We designed a deep Temporal Transformer with 4 layers, 8 attention heads, 128-dimensional embedding, and masked mean pooling. Pre-LayerNorm ensures gradient stability across long sequence backpropagation.

2. Self-Supervised Contrastive Learning (InfoNCE)

Instead of relying on noisy manual labels, the model learns self-supervised failure dynamics via InfoNCE loss (temperature τ = 0.07) using physics-preserving time-jitter and sensor jitter augmentations.

3. Discrete Signature Discovery & RUL Linear Probe

Once representations are learned, Spherical K-Means (K=4) discovers 4 discrete failure states (Nominal, Early, Accelerated, Critical). A standardized Ridge probe (α=10.0) predicts continuous Remaining Useful Life with a 130-cycle piece-wise linear cap.

4. Systematic 4-Model Ablation Matrix

Tracked in reports/results/phase3_experiments_summary.json: compared pooled vs. regime-aware normalization, explicit conditioning, and context window sizes (W=30 vs. W=50).

2. 10,000-Replicate Paired Bootstrap & Holm-Bonferroni Correction

We did not just calculate simple metrics. We performed 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples with Holm-Bonferroni Family-Wise Error Rate (FWER) step-down correction. We proved that while XGBoost has a statistically significant point MAE advantage (Δ = +2.86 cycles, p = 0.0150), FailureDNA achieves a lower NASA late-penalty score (5,607 vs 5,744) and provides unsupervised signature interpretability that decision trees cannot offer.
